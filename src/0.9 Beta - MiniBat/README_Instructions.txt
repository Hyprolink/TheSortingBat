## How to Run the Tool

Tired of digging through massive emulator and game file directories when you only use 10 of them? Me too. That's why The Sorting Bat is here!

Sounds complicated? It's not. For you, at least. Was a pain in my butt lol. Just follow the steps below:

1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click either **TheSortingBat.exe** or **FastBatBuild.exe** in the main folder.
3. Let the Sorting Bat place your paths into their rightful houses!

Which runner should you pick?
* **TheSortingBat.exe**: The full interactive sorting wizard featuring dialogue, mascot flair, and retro vibes.
* **FastBatBuild.exe**: Instant headless generation (5–8 seconds) for when you just want your folders set up immediately.

Only run one of the two executables—both build the exact same _GamingQuickAccess hub! Do you really want to sit through sorting orientation twice? Pointless!
