## How to Run the Script

Tired of digging through massive emulator file directories when you only use 10 of em, huh? Me too. That's why The Sorting Bat is here!

Sounds complicated? It's not. For you, at least. Was a pain in my butt lol. Just follow the steps below.

1. Unzip the folder.
2. Right-Click **TheSortingBat.bat**, and run it as Administrator <----- NON-NEGOTIABLE!!! Won't Work Otherwise
3. Let the Sorting Bat place your paths into their rightful houses!

Running FastBatBuild.bat inside the GottaGoFast folder instead runs faster, but you miss out on some of the fun UX!
Only use one of the two bat files, otherwise that's bad. Do you really want to attend orientation again? Pointless!
